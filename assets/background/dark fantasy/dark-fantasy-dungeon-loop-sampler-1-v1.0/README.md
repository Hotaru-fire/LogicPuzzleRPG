## Important format statement

These are static horizontal loop backgrounds. They are not layered parallax scenes, animated backgrounds, tilesets, or walkable collision maps.

The generated source images were 1672 × 941. The 1600 × 900 files are the conservative recommended runtime masters. The 1920 × 1080 files are convenience upscales and are not claimed as native generated 1920 × 1080 masters.

## Loop construction

The outer edges use a continuous sequence relocated from a natural interior cut. The surrounding edge regions are feathered back into the original scene away from the tile boundary. This makes the actual right-to-left boundary a real adjacent source transition rather than a simple mirrored or averaged line.

See `TILEABILITY_REPORT.csv` and `loop_demo.html`.
